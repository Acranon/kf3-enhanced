King's Field III Enhanced is a PC port of King's Field III, sold in North America as King's Field II (SLUS-00255).

It does not include the game. You need your own disc image of the North American King's Field II, as a .bin with its .cue (or a .chd).

The first time you start it:

  1. It asks for your disc image. Pick the .cue file.
  2. It builds the game from your disc. This takes about a minute and happens once (and again after an update).
  3. The game starts.

Your saves, settings and the built game are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\kf3-enhanced. Uninstalling does not remove them.

Settings: press F1 in the game (Settings > Classic mode, Display, Quality of life, Map...).
Quick save / load: F2 / F4.
